# Interview Storytelling Coach

A reusable AI prompt for practicing clear, credible interview answers.

## Is This Plug-and-Play?

Yes. Copy the full contents of `prompt.md` into a new AI conversation,
then provide your resume or background and a target job description
or role title.

You can complete `candidate-input-template.md` to organize your inputs,
but you do not need to rewrite the main prompt or build the scenarios
yourself.

Ask the AI to begin with the startup assessment. It should ask one
question at a time and wait for your response.

For short-answer practice, append `short-answer-drill.md` after the
main prompt and candidate inputs. This replaces the full simulation
with shorter answers, immediate feedback, and retries.

Review the briefing before starting and correct any inaccurate facts.
